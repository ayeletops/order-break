# References: original personal sites

Research done 2026-11-15 by an AI research agent. Ayelet asked for "the best and most original portfolio websites, from awards, Wix etc." as an inspiration reference.

## The lens (read this first)

This is not a mood board to copy. Read each site with one question: **what decision did the author make that no template would?** Take the decision, not the look.

Four filters, from the brief:

1. **ORDER:** is the system real (chosen measures, a reason for the grid), or a style?
2. **BREAK:** does the disruption carry something only this person could have made?
3. **ENCOUNTER:** do order and break *act on each other*, or just sit side by side?
4. **TYPOLOGY:** has this move become a template? (See the clichés list at the end.) Award sites are building a second typology next to the AI default: polished, WebGL, cursor theatre.

**Closest to my practice:** Feltron and Lupi (data as self-portrait) · Hypha (biology drives type) · Urbonas (speculative research) · Seu and Schwulst (the site as a position) · Iijima and Shai Blau (order/break as the literal structure).

**Verification key:**

- **[V]:** fetched live, or the award was seen on the awarding body's own page.
- **[V-listing]:** confirmed via a curator's page (e.g. Hoverstat.es, Wix Playground), but the site itself wasn't loaded.
- **[U]:** unverified. Check before citing.

## ORDER

**1. Feltron — Nicholas Felton** · https://feltron.com [V]
A chronological index, with the Annual Reports (2005–2014) as its spine. Each report quantifies a year of his life. A data self-portrait inside a rigid typographic system: a self-portrait can be pure metadata.

**2. Mindy Seu** · https://www.mindyseu.com [V]
One long page in a deliberately plain, unpolished style (what Olia Lialina calls the "Prof. Dr." style). The CV is a public Google Sheet. The footer shows the last update, links the previous version and says "in progress". The refusal of polish is the statement. *Anti-portfolio.*

**3. Laura Coombs** · https://www.lauracoombs.com [V]
A one-sentence bio and an email, then a single column of projects separated by rules: title, client, year. Strict hierarchy, no motion, no cards. Swiss "information first" at its limit.

**4. Elisa Breyer Archive** · https://www.elisabreyerarchive.com [V-listing · Hoverstat.es 16 Dec 2025]
An artist archive read in nested layers, zooming from overview to detail. Metadata such as colour and dimensions is shown as data. A model for "invisible systems" as layers.

**5. grid.constraint.systems — Grant Custer** · https://grid.constraint.systems [V-listing · Hoverstat.es 4 Apr 2022]
A text editor that stretches type to fill grid cells: the grid deforms the letter. A tool, not a portfolio. Raw material for an encounter.

**6. Experimental Jetset** · https://www.experimentaljetset.nl [U]
The long-running, deliberately unadorned site of a Dutch studio. Modernism as an ethic, not a style. A studio, not a person.

## BREAK

**7. Hannah Bohnen** · https://hannahbohnen.de [V-listing · Hoverstat.es 15 Oct 2024]
A printmaker's portfolio where you throw rocks at the page. The navigation is a physical game, so handwork shows up as behaviour, not decoration.

**8. Julijonas Urbonas** · https://julijonasurbonas.lt [V-listing · Hoverstat.es 11 Apr 2023, by Studio Pointer*]
An artist-researcher (*Euthanasia Coaster*). Off-kilter type, rotating navigation, dense interlinking. The closest peer to a speculative-design practice.

**9. Rafaël Rozendaal** · https://www.newrafael.com [V]
A plain index. Each one-page website is itself an artwork on its own domain, so the "portfolio" is a network of sites. The website IS the work.

**10. Lynn Fisher** · https://lynnandtonic.com [V]
A full redesign every year, with all past versions archived. The responsive breakpoints themselves become the joke. Identity as a series of versions. (Compare: my hover-to-see-iterations idea.)

**11. Christophe Guberan** · http://christopheguberan.ch [U live · Hoverstat.es 10 May 2018]
The layout balances, scales and stretches with the mouse. Content is physically elastic.

**12. Rootling** · https://rootling.place [V-listing · Hoverstat.es 28 Oct 2025]
Browsing modelled on how a pig roots: you uncover content by repeated clicks, nudges and zooms. Anti-navigation grounded in animal behaviour. *Could quorum sensing drive navigation the same way?*

**13. Hassan Kettany** · https://www.the-ihk.com [V-listing · Wix Playground Academy TLV, Nov 2024]
An homage to the old web, with a "Dev Mode" toggle. Its "Great Archive" of scanned sketchbooks, added the night before his final, got the strongest reaction. Handwork as archive, built in Wix.

## ENCOUNTER

**14. Jo Iijima** · https://joiijima.com [V · **Readymag Websites of the Year 2025, Readymag Choice**]
"A laboratory interface structured in grids", with neon colour and mutating custom type. The jury praised both its "lack of respect for the grid" and its "balance of playfulness and order". The most literal award-recognised version of my brief.

**15. Giorgia Lupi** · https://giorgialupi.com [V]
The site itself is conventional (Squarespace). The lesson is the method: Data Humanism and *Dear Data*, hand-drawn and imperfect data portraits. Handmade break applied to systematic order.

**16. Julien von Schultzendorff** · https://julienvonschultzendorff.com [V-listing · Hoverstat.es 16 Oct 2025]
A multi-column grid that stretches and squeezes, with raw-feeling type. The columns survive, pushed and pulled.

**17. Shai Blau** · https://www.shaiblau.com [V-listing · Wix Playground Academy TLV, Nov 2024]
The homepage toggles between a "random and experimental" mode and an "indexed and functional" one: "they both represent the two aspects of my identity". One typeface, two weights. ORDER/BREAK as a literal switch. *Warning: a toggle keeps them side by side. My brief asks for them to meet.*

**18. Shira Wiesenberg** · https://www.shirawiesenberg.com [V-listing · Wix Playground Academy TLV, Nov 2024]
Independently scrolling columns read as weaving, with dotted lines and type details from sewing. Textile craft becomes the grid's own logic.

**19. Laurel Schwulst** · https://laurelschwulst.com [V]
Work sorted by medium (writing, websites, worlds, teaching, talks), plus a "today" page. Her essay "My website is a shifting house…" treats the personal site as a home, not a shop window. Essential reading.

**20. Hypha — Pentagram & Counterpoint** · https://hyphahypha.com [V-listing · Hoverstat.es 10 Nov 2020]
Letterforms grown by simulated mycelium: biology breaks type, governed by an algorithm. Not personal, but the most direct precedent for a biodesigner's generative identity.

**21. *People Look at Art or Art Looks at People* — Anton Repponen** · https://space.repponen.com/peoplelookatart/ [V · **Readymag Websites of the Year 2025, Jury Choice**]
A personal photography project: a minimal, pixel-type scroll where the site seems to watch you back. A quiet system with an unsettling turn.

## TYPOLOGY-TRAPS (award winners that became templates)

**22. Bruno Simon** · https://bruno-simon.com · **Awwwards Site of the Day, 11 Nov 2019; Site of the Month, Nov 2019**
Drive a 3D toy car through the portfolio. Original in 2019, a genre now.

**23. Lusion v3** · https://lusion.co · **Awwwards Site of the Year 2023** · Webby nominee 2024
WebGL fluid and particle spectacle: the benchmark for "immersive" polish that hundreds imitate.

**24. Dennis Snellenberg** · https://dennissnellenberg.com · Awwwards Site of the Day [V-listing]
A giant name, a magnetic cursor, smooth scroll and parallax. One of the most copied freelance portfolios of the 2020s.

**25. Robin Noguier** (https://robin-noguier.com, Awwwards Site of the Day) **and Aristide Benoist** (Awwwards Site of the Month, Jun 2021)
Excellent craft: WebGL distortion, sliders, scroll transitions. Also the seed of the "award portfolio" template.

*Webby note:* portfolios go in "Professional Services & Self-Promotion". Recent winners there are agencies: Raw Materials 2024, Fiddle.Digital 2025, Righteous Creative 2026. Useful as a picture of the mainstream, not of originality.

## Clichés to avoid (the award-site typology)

1. **Cursor theatre:** a blob cursor, magnetic buttons, "View →" on hover.
2. **Smooth-scroll plus WebGL distortion:** images ripple or RGB-split; scroll is hijacked.
3. **The giant name:** an edge-to-edge grotesk or serif, plus a marquee ("Available for work ✦ Designer ✦").
4. **The preloader ritual:** a 0→100% counter, then a curtain or split-text reveal.
5. **The 3D portfolio world:** a car, a room or a floating gallery.
6. **Fluid and particle backgrounds:** iridescent blobs, noise gradients, glass. The dark cousin of the AI-default gradient.
7. **The bento case-study grid:** rounded tiles, "Selected Works (06)", "Let's work together", a local-time clock.
8. **Fake brutalism:** monospace and raw borders as a skin over a template.
