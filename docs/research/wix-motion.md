## Language

- **Type:** Madefor is the house face: geometric and minimal, with "pipe-bend" and flared terminals. Expressive moments borrow display faces per persona *[inferred]*. Prompts and typed input are treated as voice and kinetic type. No serif, slab or typewriter in the house system, so **that's my room**.
- **Colour and material:** two registers.
  1. Warm, tasteful 3D interiors for pro launches.
  2. Acid lime or neon green on black for brand and community work.
  Photography is persona-led. AI footage appears in key moments.
- **Motion:**
  - One continuous flow, not cut-to-demo.
  - UI is embedded in the scene, not beside it.
  - Bookend structure: rich scene, flat graphic middle, rich scene.
  - Split screen (2026).
  - No documented timings or easing curves.
- **Layout:** the UI is the image (cursors, pickers, pop-ups), websites shown as a poster collage, big type over full-bleed photo.

## Moves to take (for order + break)

1. **Two registers:** a calm system, with a persona register in places.
2. **Bookends:** material scenes at the start and end, flat typography in between.
3. **Type as voice:** typewriter as inner monologue against Bodoni display.
4. **UI as found graphic:** my own cursor, crop marks and selection boxes as layout.
5. **Scramble, then resolve:** a headline settles into the grid.
6. **Split screen** for process vs. result.
7. **One accent on a neutral field:** mine, not lime.
8. **Continuous transitions:** elements carry across, no hard cuts.

## Wix-house clichés to avoid

- Madefor or a wide geometric grotesk.
- Lime or neon on black.
- Beige 3D studio interiors.
- Floating UI pop-ups as hero imagery.
- Four-up mockup grids.
- Prompt-box typing animations.
- "Small business owner" persona photography.
- Self-mocking ad copy.
