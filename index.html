Who am I, if the machine can already describe me?

The modernist is the system; the anarchist is the outcome.

I feed it what it asks for. It returns an average, polite and smiling, and calls it me. I let it. Between the prompt and the answer there is a gap, and I keep it open on purpose.

Some things live only inside me. They have no file, no caption, no training data. Here you will find their outlines, never their contents.

The shell can be read. What moves inside it cannot.

How do you use the machine without being devoured by it? Give it the grid. Keep the ghost.
